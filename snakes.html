If someone is bitten

From the Health Ministry's Standard Treatment Guidelines: Snakebite (June 2016), first aid by bystanders:

  1. Reassure the person: most snakebites are from non-venomous snakes, and panic speeds up the heart.
  2. Keep the bitten arm or leg still, like a broken limb, with a splint along its length. Do not block the blood supply or apply pressure.
  3. Nothing to eat or drink until they reach a health centre.
  4. Traditional remedies have no proven benefit.
  5. Take them to the nearest PHC or hospital at once. They must not run or drive themselves.
  6. Do not waste time trying to kill or catch the snake.

Do not use tight tourniquets, cutting or sucking the wound, electric shocks, ice, or scarification.
